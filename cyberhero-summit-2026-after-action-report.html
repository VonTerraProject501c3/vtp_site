The third Summit in numbers: who came, who spoke and who backed it.

Event 2026-10-23 Venue Microsoft Garage, Reston, Virginia Public edition of the report prepared 2026-11-03

01

Three years of the Summit

Elisha Angeles-Ashook founded CyberHERo in 2024 and held the first Summit that December. Each year since, the program has grown, and Microsoft has hosted all three (CyberHERo, n.d.-a).

2024

Inaugural Summit

2024-12-13, Microsoft Reston

  • Keynote by Diana Kelley on the leadership gap
  • KC7 hands-on investigation
  • 43 one-on-one career conversations
  • Headshots and a social hour
2025

Second annual

2025-10-24, Microsoft Reston

  • More than 100 unique registrants
  • WiCyS keynote by Mansi Chaturvedi
  • Ask the CISO panel
  • Workshops by KC7, Microsoft and SANS; a sponsor expo
2026

Third annual

2026-10-23, Microsoft Garage, Reston

  • 132 registrations, up from more than 100, and 150 people on the day
  • 20 speakers, 2 panels, 6 breakout sessions
  • 4 sponsors and 4 community partners
  • 52 professional headshots

02

The program and the speakers

A full day on the main stage and in breakout rooms, from 08:30 to the social hour at 17:00. Speakers came from Microsoft, NIST, SANS, GIAC, Bloomberg, Micron, MajorKey, KC7, Expel, EnQode, Meridian Capability Group, ALN Leadership and Chaos & Signal (CyberHERo, n.d.-c).

What attendees said

Seven people answered the feedback form. That is a small sample, so read it as a signal: nobody rated any part of the day below Good, and six of seven rated the event overall Excellent (CyberHERo, 2026b).

Carry into 2027

Attendees asked for three things: a seated lunch area so nobody eats alone, parking guidance in the confirmation email, and notice ahead of time that a photographer will be there. One also noted that breakouts sometimes overlapped mentor slots.

How we checked this

This is the public edition of the After Action Report prepared for CyberHERo and VTP leadership on 2026-11-03. It draws on the CyberHERo 2026 ledger (registrations, passes and donations), the headshot contact sheet (52 people) and the seven feedback responses received by 2026-11-03. The agenda, speakers, sponsors and past Summits were read on cyberherocommunity.com on 2026-11-03. The headcount of 150 counts speakers, volunteers and attendees; Microsoft, as host, confirmed it with Elisha Angeles-Ashook on 2026-11-04. Speakers are named as published on cyberherocommunity.com.

The full report, with spending, funding sources and the items still being closed out, stays with CyberHERo and VTP leadership. This edition leaves out vendor-level costs, donor and registrant breakdowns and attendee comments. No attendee names or email addresses appear here.
