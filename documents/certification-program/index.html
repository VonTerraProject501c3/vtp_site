Cybersecurity Certification Program

A free, needs-based pathway to industry certification. Paired with a mentor, start to finish.

The Von Terra Project's Cybersecurity Certification Program (P1) removes the financial and accessibility barriers that keep capable people out of the cybersecurity workforce. Every candidate is paired with a mentor and a structured roadmap, from application through their first industry credential. At no cost.

Every candidate starts at entry level, on a certification aligned to the DoD 8140 Cyberspace Workforce Qualification Program (which superseded the older DoD 8570 issuance). Which specific certification depends on the direction you're heading. Your Certification Program Manager maps that during onboarding: see Certification Tracks below for the paths VTP funds.

The Candidate Journey

01 Apply Submit the Certification Program Intake Form.
02 Select Scored on need; selection runs on published dates, limited by funding and mentor capacity.
03 Onboard Get paired with a mentor, then complete the Mutual Commitment Agreement together at kickoff.
04 Study Progress through milestones to the Go/No-Go practice-exam gate.
05 Certify Sit the sponsored exam, then advance or apply to become a mentor.

How Candidates Are Selected

Weighted Intake Scoring

Applications are scored out of 100 points across three weighted categories, so the program prioritizes candidates facing the greatest financial and social need.

No Minimum Score

Applications are ranked on need, not measured against a cutoff. Candidates not selected in a round roll forward to the next one rather than having to reapply.

Funding and Mentors Both Set the Size

How many candidates VTP takes is limited by the funding available for exam vouchers and by the capacity our mentor pool has offered. Whichever is smaller governs. We don't select more candidates than we can both fund and mentor.

Applications Stay Open

There is no window to miss. Selection runs on published dates, and everything received before a selection date is considered in that round. Selected candidates have 10 business days to confirm before an alternate is contacted.

Certification Tracks

Three entry-level paths. Your Certification Program Manager maps you to the one that fits during onboarding.

IT Support

CompTIA A+, then Network+. The classic on-ramp into help desk, technical support, and system administration work.

Cyber Defense

ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity, then GIAC's GFACT and GISF. Aimed at cyber defense analyst and security-operations work.

Incident Response

GIAC's GCIH. A stretch step after Cyber Defense, not a first purchase: it costs more and opens a narrower set of entry-level roles on its own.

All three tracks stay at DoD 8140's Basic proficiency level, chosen because each has no hard prerequisite and a documented path for VTP to fund it directly. Certifications like CompTIA Security+ sit at DoD's Intermediate level and are a strong second certification once a track is complete, not where VTP starts a candidate.

Apply

Applications are reviewed each cohort cycle. There's no cost to apply, and no cost to participate. VTP provides study materials and a mentor to every selected candidate, and sponsors the exam voucher once the candidate clears the Go/No-Go practice-exam gate.
