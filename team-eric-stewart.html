Eric Stewart

President

Eric James Stewart is a retired Army musician, intelligence professional, cyber operations support manager, and Board President of The Von Terra Project. His career revolved around identifying and solving problems. Eric retired from the US Army in JAN 2024 after 29 years of Active and National Guard military service. After a tour with Task Force Echo 4 in 2020 he began working for Marine Corps Cyberspace Command as a DOD Civilian in the operations support department, until June 2024. While currently traveling on sabbatical, he enjoys exploring the links between intelligence, processes, and cyber operations.

Eric’s expertise is linking operational goals to internal processes. He excels in identifying questions that need to be answered to achieve those goals, as well as mismatches between stated goals and implemented policies, to align goals and internal processes.

  • Bachelor of Science in Political Science — Middle Tennessee State University, Murfreesboro, TN
  • All-Source Intelligence Warrant Officer Advanced and Basic Course

Passion & Mission
