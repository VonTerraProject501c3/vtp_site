Programs

These are The Von Terra Project’s six programs, as set out in our organizational charter and approved by the board. Each carries its own objective and deliverables across our three pillars (people, policy, and technology), and each maps to one or more of our Lines of Effort.

Looking for something practical to use right now? Those live under Tools.

[P1] Cybersecurity Certification Program

L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L6

A structured, resource-rich pathway for candidates to achieve industry-standard cybersecurity credentials, removing financial and accessibility barriers for people entering the cybersecurity workforce. Includes candidate intake and evaluation, curated certification tracks, a peer study hub, and full sponsorship of exam vouchers and study materials for qualified candidates.

Certification Program

[P2] Cybersecurity Mentorship Program

L1, L2, L3, L5, L6

Connects experienced cybersecurity professionals with candidates to provide ground-up guidance, translate complex technical concepts into usable skills, and build a stronger, more resilient cyber workforce through active professional relationships. Mentors initiate the relationship; both sides sign a mutual commitment agreement.

Learn more

[P3] Professional Development & Engagement

L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L6

Fosters continuous professional growth and industry leadership through financial support for symposia, specialized training pathways, and structured knowledge sharing, including a train-the-trainer model so what one member learns reaches the whole community.

Learn more

[P4] Grant Initiative

L1, L3, L4, L5, L6

A strategic funding outreach and distribution program that secures grants, corporate sponsorships, and financial resources for nonprofits and small businesses, enabling them to fortify defenses and build sustainable resilience. Includes the VTP microgrant program and grant-writing standards.

Learn more

[P5] Digital Footprint & Communal Defense

L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L6

A unified ecosystem combining personal digital footprint management with a collaborative nationwide network, providing the defense-in-depth tools and expert personnel required to secure individuals and organizations. Home of the security self-assessment tool and the resource library.

Learn more

[P6] CyberHERo

Women in Cyber Empowerment Initiative · L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L6

Empowers, inspires, and elevates women in cybersecurity by fostering mentorship, building inclusive networks, and increasing access to education and leadership pathways, ensuring a diverse and resilient talent pipeline.

Visit CyberHERo

People → L2, L5

Every program’s People work builds the cohort and cultivates talent. That is why mentorship and professional development sit at the centre of the organization rather than off to one side.

Policy → L4, L6

Every program’s Policy work builds cross-sector partnerships and feeds the policy matrix. The standards we write for ourselves are the ones we recommend outward.

Technology → L1, L3

Every program’s Technology work feeds the informational hub and the training environment. Tools built for one program become resources for all.

That pattern is not a coincidence. It is the People · Policy · Technology lens applied consistently. Read across the matrix to see what a program delivers; read down to see everything advancing one outcome.

[L1] Create an informational hub for cyber-related issues, educational material, and emergency management resources.

Target: A self-assessment tool used by 100 organizations or individuals by 2028, and an emergency preparedness guide with at least 10 scenario-based response plans by 2027.
