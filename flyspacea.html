FlySpaceA

In partnership with The Von Terra Project

What is FlySpaceA?

FlySpaceA is a Space-A flight tracking service for the military community. Space-A ("Space Available") travel lets service members, retirees, veterans, and their families fly free on Department of Defense aircraft when seats go unused. The schedules have always been the hard part. They’re scattered across terminal pages, often stale, and hard to trust. FlySpaceA watches official Air Mobility Command terminal schedules around the clock and tells travelers the moment a seat shows up, on the web and on mobile, free.

About 11.5 million Americans have earned this benefit. In a typical year, roughly 1% manage to use it. Closing that gap is why FlySpaceA exists.

How VTP and FlySpaceA Help Each Other

The Von Terra Project and FlySpaceA partner to put a capability built by veterans into the hands of the service members, retirees, veterans, and families who earned it. We build it with the security, privacy, and OPSEC discipline that community deserves.

How FlySpaceA Supports VTP

FlySpaceA gives VTP a working, at-scale example of its mission: people, policy, and technology brought together for a community that needs it. It reaches roughly 10,000 registered travelers as of September 2026, and a combined social following in the tens of thousands. It also puts VTP’s security principles into practice rather than onto a slide. FlySpaceA publishes no CENTCOM flights. It stores no tail numbers or aircraft identifiers, and it keeps the minimum data required to deliver an alert. That is the standard VTP advocates for, running in production.

How VTP Supports FlySpaceA

The Von Terra Project supports FlySpaceA in three ways, none of them financial. It advises on cybersecurity, privacy, and policy, drawing on the expertise of its board and volunteer network. It promotes the service to the military community through its reach and relationships. And it places graduates of its cybersecurity programs as systems administrators on the platform, giving them production experience serving the community many of them come from. The Von Terra Project does not fund FlySpaceA and pays none of its costs. The goal is shared: get this tool in front of every eligible traveler, and ultimately into the hands of Air Mobility Command.

Read the numbers behind the partnership in the FlySpaceA Community Impact Report, or reach out to learn more.
