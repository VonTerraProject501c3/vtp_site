Elisha Angeles-Ashook

Vice President

Elisha Angeles-Ashook is a cybersecurity leader specializing in threat-informed defense, cyber resilience, and security operations. As the Capability Lead for Assessments & Exercises at Microsoft, she designs and executes strategic cyber engagements, helping organizations proactively strengthen their security posture against advanced threats.

With expertise in red teaming, threat hunting, and cloud security, Elisha has led high-impact assessments across government, defense, and enterprise environments. She has been instrumental in developing advanced security capabilities, including cloud-based cyber ranges, threat-informed cyber exercises, and tailored security assessments.

Elisha is committed to mentorship and workforce development, helping to shape the next generation of cybersecurity professionals. Through her nonprofit work, she develops programs, training initiatives, and community-driven efforts that empower aspiring cybersecurity professionals and create opportunities for underrepresented groups in the field.

Her work in cybersecurity and nonprofits is focused on collaboration, education, and resilience, ensuring that organizations and individuals alike are equipped to face the evolving challenges of the digital age.

Passion & Mission

  • Empowering individuals and organizations to build stronger cyber resilience strategies
  • Bridging the gap between technical expertise and real-world application
  • Creating opportunities for underrepresented groups in cybersecurity
