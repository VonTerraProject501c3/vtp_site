General Questions

The Von Terra Project strives to bridge the gap between people, policy, and technology to connect communities, build cybersecurity resilience, strengthen preparedness, optimize response, and fortify our collective defenses against emerging challenges. In practice that means free training, free certification sponsorship, and practical security guidance for people and organizations that cannot buy it. The Von Terra Project is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

A 501(c)(3) organization is a tax-exempt nonprofit recognized by the IRS. Donations to our organization may be tax-deductible, and we operate for charitable purposes.

You can support us by making a donation, volunteering, attending our events, or spreading awareness about our mission.
