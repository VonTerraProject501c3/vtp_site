General Questions

The Von Terra Project is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to proactively, actively, and reactively addressing cyber-related challenges to people, policy, and technology for the public, organizational clients, students, and emergency response teams to ensure a grounded and secure future.

A 501(c)(3) organization is a tax-exempt nonprofit recognized by the IRS. Donations to our organization may be tax-deductible, and we operate for charitable purposes.

You can support us by making a donation, volunteering, attending our events, or spreading awareness about our mission.
